# Session 2026-08-30

Scope: Explain how furin strike intervals are determined.

- Topic: Strike timing
  - Decision: Document the initial delay, wind-dependent stochastic interval,
    per-chime minimum gap, and occasional bounce strike.
  - Rationale: The perceived interval is produced by several layers in
    `scene.py` and `wind.py`, rather than one fixed timer.

- Topic: Sustained wind bursts
  - Decision: Pre-generate interval fluctuation values in batches of 100 and
    adjust the interval ranges so their short ends can fall below one second.
  - Rationale: Low-frequency 1/f runs can create clustered strikes without a
    separate hand-authored wind-on state.
  - Result: Breeze and moderate presets can now reach sub-second intervals;
    the short side now reaches the configured 0.1-second floor, and the full
    30-test suite passes.

- Topic: Continuous-strike dynamics
  - Decision: Remove separate bounce events and soften the second and later
    strikes in a sequence whose actual gaps are below 0.3 seconds.
  - Rationale: A sustained wind burst should sound like one continuous event,
    rather than a normal strike plus an independent bounce probability.

- Topic: Continuous-strike length
  - Decision: Do not enforce a hard sequence-length limit.
  - Rationale: The 1/f-driven variation should remain probabilistic; the
    distribution adjustment is recorded below.

- Topic: Interval distribution
  - Decision: Reshape only the negative side of the generated 1/f values with
    a fourth-power transform before converting them to intervals.
  - Rationale: Reduce extreme short-gap values without breaking the temporal
    correlation of the 1/f sequence or imposing a hard run-length limit.
